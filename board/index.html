Badges

    Scan a badge to put it on the board.

    1. On the badge, open CTF and choose Leaderboard.
    2. Press SELECT twice, past Teams, to reach SCAN ME.
    3. Point your phone's camera at the QR code and open the link.

    About this board

    It stays on your phone. A scan carries a badge's name, score, flags and team, plus an id made from a hash. They ride in the link after the #, which your browser never sends to the website. The board is kept in this browser and goes nowhere else.

    Bragging rights only. It trusts badges the way the badges trust each other. Scores that don't add up are refused, and anything worth a prize gets checked in person.

    Works without signal. After the first visit the page loads offline, so scanning works in a basement hall. On Android you can also add it to the home screen. On an iPhone, keep it in Safari: that's where a scanned link opens.
