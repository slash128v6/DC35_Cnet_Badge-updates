Updates

Badges update themselves from this repository: about every ten minutes when they have Wi-Fi and automatic updates are on, or straight away from Settings → Updates → Check now.

A badge installs nothing it cannot verify. It checks the release's signature against keys built into its own firmware, and the download against the signed hash, before writing anything.

On firmware 1.0.20 or older? Those look for updates at an address that no longer serves them, and need one update over USB: from your browser, or with the cable kit if you'd rather use a command line. After that they update over Wi-Fi again.

Published with firmware 1.0.32. This site is rebuilt with every release.
